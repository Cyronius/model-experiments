## Why this round

The live pipeline is a shared uncentered PCA projection of Qwen3.5-2B's
residual stream (2,048 → 1,520; 74.35% of stored scalars), followed by LoRA
correctness healing. The newest results point at three problems:

- **The healing recipe costs skills even without projection.** In the matched
  capacity study, stock Qwen3.5-2B falls 19/39 → 14/39 (rank 16) and → 17/39
  (rank 64) under the same curriculum
  ([capacity-projection-20261026](results/capacity-projection-20261026/README.md)).
  Changing rank does not address this.
- **The projection starts badly, and residual cuts compound.** The unhealed
  projection scores 6/39 against stock's 19/39. In an older, harsher setting
  (2B, PCA width 512, not the live 1,520 model), single-site projection cost
  0.13–0.73 nats while all 49 sites together cost 3.18 nats
  ([findings.md](findings.md)); those numbers show compounding, not the live
  model's loss. Calibration breadth alone moved the closed
  score 8/56 → 24/56 with no training
  ([local-projection-20261025](results/local-projection-20261025/README.md)),
  so geometry choices have effects comparable to healing.
- **Evaluation sets are too small to separate arms.** Recent comparisons turn
  on differences of one to five answers out of 39–56. Paired comparisons
  depend on discordant counts, and these are few.

Throughput constrains everything. The capacity study's logs show about
10.5k input tokens per update and 250–325 s per update on CPU (roughly
35–40 tokens/s with four threads and the GatedDeltaNet torch fallback). That
is an observation under shared-resource conditions, not a benchmark; a clean
measurement under `dibs` comes before any run plan.

## A. Primary: retention-aware healing objective (Codex)

Starting point: the development-selected parent from
[curriculum-healing-20261026](results/curriculum-healing-20261026/README.md),
expanded to rank 64 by a function-preserving expansion (alpha/rank constant,
new A rows seeded, new B columns zero; B is out_features × rank).

Expansion gate, before any training: logit tolerance against the parent,
cache consistency, and exact fixed baseline generations. Padding can change
floating-point reductions, so the gate is a tolerance, not bitwise equality.

Arms, with identical answer tokens and update counts and fresh AdamW in every
trained arm:

| Arm | Model | Training |
|---|---|---|
| A0 | projected parent, unchanged | none (stays eligible for selection) |
| A1 | projected, rank 64 | revised verified SFT plus replay |
| A2 | projected, rank 64 | the same SFT plus a forward-KL retention term |
| S1 | stock, rank 64 | as A1 |
| S2 | stock, rank 64 | as A2 |

Out of the first screen: pure KD, rank-16 continuation, rank sweeps, rank
truncation, on-policy or reverse KL.

Curriculum revisions for A1/A2: broader verified replay, varied wording
(including word-problem forms for arithmetic, which is how it is tested), and
less repetition of the small science pool (32 responses currently take 20% of
answer tokens).

KD specification (agreed; details frozen by Codex in `retention_protocol.json`):

- Direction KL(teacher ‖ student); the teacher is the pinned stock source.
- Temperature 1 and loss weight λ = 1, as pilot choices, not claimed optimal.
- The KL term applies **only to explicitly tagged verified retention and
  replay rows**, not to newly taught skills. This isolates retention and
  avoids pulling new skills toward known source errors.
- Either exact full-vocabulary KL computed in chunks, online, or cached top-k
  teacher probabilities **plus a tail bucket** for the omitted mass. Never
  renormalize top-k and call it full KL. Top-k plus tail is a coarsened lower
  bound on the full KL; report its error against exact full KL on a sample.
- The teacher's input distribution stated explicitly. No evaluation answers
  are cached or used.
- The protocol blocks model runs until data manifests, numerical thresholds
  and local runtime bounds exist and have been reviewed.

## B. Secondary: one allocation pilot (Claude)

Question: at the same stored-scalar budget, is it less destructive to cut the
residual stream less and prune MLP neurons instead? Residual cuts reach every
site and compound; MLP pruning is local and can be refit by least squares.
Earlier MLP-only builds are motivation for this, not evidence: their source,
settings and output-norm handling differ.

| Arm | Residual width | MLP width | Budget |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| P0 | 1,520 (current shared-broad, reproduced) | 6,144 | 74.35% |
| P1 | 1,792 | pruned to match P0 | 74.35% |

- Same source revision, broad 64k calibration mixture and decoding as P0.
- Stored scalars counted identically for both arms: the output matrix norm,
  tied vocabulary counted once, and any refit overhead.
- MLP pruning uses importance ranking plus least-squares refit of the down
  projection, as in `build_pruned.py`, reimplemented in a new file against
  the current shared modules.
- Controls: full-width P1 settings (no residual cut, no pruning) must
  reproduce the source; P0 must reproduce the existing shared-broad model.

P1 is judged unhealed first, on the fresh suite from A **and** the cheap
metrics below. It must pass the generation safeguards (code, long-form,
termination), not only the cheap metrics. Only if it clears its gate does it
receive the same continuation as A's winner. The unhealed matched comparison
can establish an unhealed difference; any claim that the allocation helps
after healing needs the healed comparison.

## Measurement

**0a, generation suite (Codex; selection authority).** Fresh group-disjoint
development and test sets; per-family correct→wrong and wrong→correct counts
with denominators; code edge and property tests; a science factual rubric
scored blind to arm where possible; historical test sets become
regression-only. Selection uses development only, the parent stays eligible,
and retention, code and termination constraints are separate rather than
pooled into one sum. Practical-effect and no-code-loss thresholds are frozen
before any new output is seen. Stratified seen and held-out generation plus
teacher-forced CE diagnose fit versus generalization.

**0b, teacher-forced metrics (Claude; secondary diagnostics, reviewed by
Codex).** `tf_metrics.py`:

- Held-out teacher KL, top-1 agreement and teacher-forced CE on a broad
  held-out mixture, disjoint from all calibration and training text.
- Log-likelihood multiple choice on fixed subsets, with choice tokenization
  and length normalization stated explicitly.
- Paired arm differences with bootstrap by task group. Related items are not
  counted as independent samples.
- Sanity: stock scores KL 0; run on the unhealed shared-broad model, the
  parent and the selected adapter.

These metrics never select a checkpoint on their own. Every candidate
considered for selection also goes through the 0a generation gates.

## C. Deferred, with triggers

  injected-projector equivalence passes exact full-width and
  rectangular-versus-existing-builder controls (covering the compressed RMS

  and the cache). Backpropagation still traverses the whole model, and a
  3.1M-parameter basis can overfit a small corpus, so this is not cheap.
- **Broader KD** (more text, pure KD arms): only if A2 beats A1 on retention
  without a correctness loss.
- Other widths, gradient-weighted PCA, nonlinear decoders, per-layer bases,
  token-dependent RMS: parked.
- ROCm on the Strix Halo GPU: a separate, bounded infrastructure task, not a
  prerequisite.
